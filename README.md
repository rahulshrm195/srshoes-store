# SR Shoes — online store (srshoes.in)

Customer shop for SR Shoes, Nashik. One file: `index.html`.

- Stock comes from the catalog app (catalog-srshoe). Every stock import or "Publish to catalog" refreshes it.
- Orders go to the shop on WhatsApp; staff confirm and take payment by UPI.
- Settings (WhatsApp number, delivery charge, exchange days, shop address and map) are at the top of `index.html` under `STORE`.
